Some tricks of the trade for the breakout exercises (1 of 2)
For the breakout exercises, you’ll be in multidisciplinary teams.
▪ Make sure you know who knows what.
Identify on your team:
▪ A scribe
▪ A timekeeper
▪ A presenter
▪ A decision-maker
• Important: “Debate which questions to ask, not the answers.”
Instructors will visit every team. Use them:
▪ Just as instructors and mentors
▪ "In role" as Case Study expert users
Remember this about the exercise presentations that you will prepare:
▪ There will be a presentation and discussion.
▪ You will probably use 3 - 4 working flip chart sheets or screenshots per team.
▪ We’ll call on one or more teams each session.
▪ We’ll discuss a “model answer” at the end.

Case study Exercise 1: Requirements Definition
At the end of this exercise, you will need to be ready with the following:
▪ A System Context Diagram:
▪ Remember to include both human and system actors
▪ A high-level Use Case Model (a “bubble diagram”)
▪ Do not include Use Cases themselves
▪ List of key qualities (non-functional requirements) that the solution needs to satisfy
