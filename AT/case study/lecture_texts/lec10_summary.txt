Course objectives: rear mirror
At the beginning of this course, we said that this course would enable you to:
• Describe architecture.
• Understand how to elicit and analyze requirements.
• Define and classify nonfunctional requirements.
• Explain an Architecture Overview and how it fits with the Component and Operational Models.
• Define architectural decisions.
• Understand and apply architectural principles, such as loose coupling and strong cohesion.
• Identify the functional aspects of an IT architecture.
• Explain the approach, steps and artifacts used to define an Operational Model.
• Describe technical reviews and how to incorporate full life cycle validation and verification into
architectural thinking.
• Understand how architects operate within agile teams and how agile frameworks and
approaches affect architectural styles.

All senior roles involve a critical nontechnical skill: Leadership
Architects are important leaders on any project.
Study topics in leadership:
• Books on leadership can usually be found in the business section of your local bookstores.
• Read autobiographies of leaders to learn their leadership techniques.
• Courses in leadership are available (in Your Learning and external learning platforms).
• Use IBM Leadership Academy materials
Becoming an effective leader requires a commitment to lifelong learning.
To be a leader is to be a teacher.
